# Facts used in the book, with sources

Every real-world claim in the book is listed here before it is used
(`STYLE.md`, section 3). One entry per fact:

- **ID**: F001, F002, ... Cite it in the chapter source as a LaTeX comment,
  `% F012`, next to the sentence that uses it.
- **Claim**: one plain sentence, exactly as precise as the book will be.
- **Source**: URL(s) or bibliographic reference; prefer primary and
  authoritative sources.
- **Checked**: date, and how (read the source / secondary only).
- **Confidence**: high, medium, low. Low means: do not use, or attribute it
  ("according to ...").

Entries are added as research is done. Facts that turned out wrong are not
deleted: they are struck through with a note, so the history is visible.

### F001 Welch's Flintstones jelly glasses
- **Claim**: In the early 1960s (glasses dated 1962, 1963 and 1964) Welch's
  sold grape jelly in glass tumblers printed with Flintstones characters,
  about 4¼ inches tall, which families reused as children's juice glasses.
- **Source**: Heritage Auctions lot, "The Flintstones Welch's Grape Jelly
  Glass Tumblers Full Case of 24 (Hanna-Barbera/Welch's, 1962)",
  https://comics.ha.com/itm/memorabilia/miscellaneous/the-flintstones-welch-s-grape-jelly-glass-tumblers-full-case-of-24-hanna-barbera-welch-s-1962-/a/40237-84048.s ;
  WorthPoint listings, e.g.
  https://www.worthpoint.com/worthopedia/flintstones-welchs-jelly-glasses-1962-1900563318
- **Checked**: 2026-10-01, search summaries of collector and auction
  listings (secondary).
- **Confidence**: medium. Dates and size from collector listings; good
  enough for a memory, not for a precise historical claim.

### F002 Pruning season in Napa
- **Claim**: In Napa, vines are dormant in winter and are pruned between
  late December and March; many growers wait until February or March,
  because rain carries fungal spores (notably Eutypa) into fresh pruning
  cuts, and January is used to mark the wood to cut or keep.
- **Source**: UC Master Gardeners of Sacramento County, "In the vineyard",
  https://ucanr.edu/site/uc-master-gardeners-sacramento-county/vineyard ;
  Napa Valley Register, "The all-important pruning dominates in the late
  winter vineyard",
  https://napavalleyregister.com/wine/the-all-important-pruning-dominates-in-the-late-winter-vineyard/article_b3eb35a4-4123-5f78-883f-68594374d999.html
- **Checked**: 2026-10-01, search summaries (secondary).
- **Confidence**: medium-high. Read the UC page before quoting it.
