## Chapter plans

### Chapter 1 (plan, 2026-10-01)

Time of writing, inside the fiction: January 2020. Movement I (Order).
Working title: "Concord".

Movements of the chapter:

1. The grape, encyclopedically: what it is, botanically; the genus; how many
   kinds; how many tonnes. He buys a bunch of grapes in January (imported,
   out of season) and counts its berries. The count does not come out the
   same twice.
2. The first grape he knew was not a grape: Welch's grape jelly in a small
   Iowa town he will not name. The jar, the glass it became, the hardware
   store. The taste Americans call "grape" is the taste of one variety, the
   Concord, and of one molecule.
3. The Concord and its maker: Ephraim Bull, the seedling, the epitaph.
   Welch, the dentist, the communion juice. (Notes and subnotes; citations.)
4. Iowa had grapes after all: the vineyards before Prohibition; the wild
   riverbank grape along the creeks. He must have walked past them. A
   sentence that does not finish.
5. Now: Napa, January, the vines dormant, the wood marked for pruning. He
   marks his with tape, because the serpette is not where it should be. The
   pond outside the window. He does not drink wine any more; this is not a
   book about wine.

Asides (margin) in this chapter: the method (Card 1, the date and the
minute; the shoeboxes; the typewriter, "a gift"); one or two that do not fit
their line (the time of night; the level of the water); one "you".

Tells (`STYLE.md`, 4.2), at most two or three, each with an innocent
reading: the count that changes; the town he will not name; the sentence
that breaks off at the creek; the missing serpette, mentioned as an
inconvenience.

Facts: only from `book/research/FACTS.md`, each cited in the source with
`% Fnnn`.
