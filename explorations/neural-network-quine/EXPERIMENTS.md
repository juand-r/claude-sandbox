# Experiments: definitions and registry of every run

This file says what every run was, precisely enough to repeat it. The findings
and their interpretation are in REPORT.md; ideas not yet tested are in IDEAS.md;
the day-to-day log, including mistakes, is in NOTES.md.

## 1. The model

A quine network takes the index c of one of its own weights and outputs a
guess f(c) for that weight.

- θ: all trained weights, one flat vector of length N.
- P: a fixed random table with one row per weight, never trained, not part of θ.
  Row c, written p_c, is weight c's "address" (100 numbers).
- σ: the SELU activation function, applied element by element.

Two architectures:

| name | formula | trained matrices | N |
|---|---|---|---|
| two layers (the paper's) | f(c) = wᵀ σ(W₂ σ(W₁ σ(p_c))) | W₁, W₂ (100 × 100), w (100) | 20,100 |
| one layer | f(c) = wᵀ σ(W₁ σ(p_c)) | W₁ (100 × 100), w (100) | 10,100 |

The MNIST version (paper reproduction only) splits the first layer into 50 units
from the address and 50 from a fixed random projection of an image, and adds a
100 × 10 classifier head (N = 21,100).

## 2. Measures

- SSE (sum of squared errors) = Σ_c (f(c) − θ_c)². The paper calls it L_SR.
- R² = 1 − SSE / Σ_c (θ_c − mean θ)². 1 is a perfect copy; 0 is no better than
  guessing the mean weight; negative is worse than that. This is the measure of
  success. SSE alone can be lowered by shrinking the weights.
- Weight RMS = √(mean θ_c²), the typical size of a weight.

## 3. The dimensions along which runs differ (registry columns)

| column | values | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| layers | 1, 2 | number of trained 100 × 100 hidden matrices |
| weight init | default, He | default: PyTorch's nn.Linear rule, uniform on ±0.1 (std 0.058); He: normal with std √(2/100) = 0.141 |
| P scale | 0.058, 1 | standard deviation of the entries of P (normal distribution) |
| SELU on embedding | yes, no | whether σ is applied to p_c before W₁ |
| training | SGD, Adam, Adagrad, Adamax, RMSprop, hill-climbing, regeneration, damped Newton, pure Newton | see section 4 |
| loss | SSE, 1 − R² | what the training minimizes (MNIST runs add a classification term) |
| targets | frozen, live | frozen: the targets are a copy of θ taken at the start of each epoch, and the gradient only moves the outputs; live (full gradient): the targets are θ itself, so the gradient also moves each weight toward its prediction |
| lr | default, number | learning rate; Adamax default is 2e-3 |
| length | epochs (ep), iterations (it), generations (gen) | one epoch visits every weight once, in minibatches of 10 |
| started from | random init, a saved network | continuation runs start from a saved network |

## 4. Training methods

- Gradient optimizers (paper): minibatches of 10 weights, frozen targets unless
  stated, PyTorch defaults except SGD lr 0.01 and SGD momentum 0.9.
- Hill-climbing (paper): per minibatch, add Gaussian noise of size σ to every
  weight; keep the change if the minibatch SSE falls.
- Regeneration (paper): replace every weight by the network's guess of it, after
  T epochs of Adamax; repeated G times.
- Pure Newton: solve (J − I) Δ = −r for the residual r = f(C) − θ, with J the
  Jacobian of the outputs with respect to the weights. Fails here (REPORT.md).
- Damped Newton (Levenberg–Marquardt): Δ = −(AᵀA + μI)⁻¹ Aᵀ r with A = J − I and
  a damping μ that adapts after each accepted or rejected step. With the 1 − R²
  objective, r and A are replaced by r/√S and (A − r (θ − mean θ)ᵀ / S)/√S, where
  S = Σ(θ − mean θ)².

## 5. Saved networks and where continuation runs started

Every run's final network is in `results/weights/<run>.pt`: the weights θ, the
settings that regenerate P, and a fingerprint of P. Load with
`quine.load_weights(path)`, which fails if the regenerated P does not match.

Continuation runs started from short-named copies of earlier runs
(`results/checkpoint_provenance.txt`):

| copy | is the final network of |
|---|---|
| stage2_L1_seed{s} | one layer, He, P 1: 1,000 ep at lr 2e-3, then 300 at 2e-4, then 300 at 2e-5 (frozen) |
| stage3_L1_seed{s} | stage2_L1_seed{s} + 300 ep full gradient at 2e-5 |
| stage2_L2_seed{s} | two layers, He, P 1: 1,000 ep at lr 2e-3, then 300 at 2e-4, then 300 at 2e-5 (frozen) |
