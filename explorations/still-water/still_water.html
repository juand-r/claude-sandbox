Garden words and network words
Spring
an input, x
Gate stone
a weight, w. Teal channels add water downstream; plum channels draw it away (negative weights).
Middle pool
a hidden unit; its water level is the unit's activation
Basin
the output, the prediction ŷ
Natural level
the target, y
Ripple height
the loss, L = ½ Σ (ŷ − y)²
Listen
the backward pass: ripples travel upstream
A gate's ripple
∂L/∂w: the arrow shows which way the gate should move, its size how strongly
A breath
one step of gradient descent, w ← w − η ∂L/∂w, for every gate at once
Breath depth
the learning rate, η

Drag a gate stone up or down, or focus it and use the arrow keys.
