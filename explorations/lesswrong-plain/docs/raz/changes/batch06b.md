
## annotated/afterwords/final-words.tex

Why: Fiction: the teacher's "it was you who failed your Art" is a character's creed with a reading as advice the notes give; kept in the Response, out of the In short line. The scope point is phrased as for The Ritual and Class Project.

- Before: In short: a well-made farewell whose second half acts out its first and suggests an answer to its own closing question; its central claim, that mastery comes through failure, is foretold rather than shown, and its rule that every failure is the student's is stated as fact with no way to check it in a given case.
- After: In short: a well-made farewell whose second half acts out its first and suggests an answer to its own closing question; like ``The Ritual'' and ``Class Project,'' it stops before the failure through which, it says, mastery comes.
