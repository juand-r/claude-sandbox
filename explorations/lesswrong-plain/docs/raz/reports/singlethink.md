# Report: singlethink ("Singlethink", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-10-06)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

At fifteen the author realized that a pleasing childhood memory, of refusing a camp spanking game out of a principled refusal to hurt others and to play a negative-sum game, was untrue: the child had refused only to avoid being hurt, and the author had always known this and looked away from it. Having caught the feeling of pushing an unwanted truth into a corner of the mind, the author resolved to notice it every time and named the skill "singlethink", after Orwell's doublethink: notice that you are forgetting, and remember. The mechanisms of confirmation bias go deep, but this first skill cleared many such corners.
