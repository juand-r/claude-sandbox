# Report: your-price-for-joining ("Your Price for Joining", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-26)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

In the Ultimatum Game people reject low offers even at real cost, and the post suggests an
analogue in joining group projects: a would-be member withholds support until the group meets
some demand, and people in the "atheist/libertarian/technophile/sf-fan" cluster, it seems to the
author, set that price far too high. The post's explanation is hunter-gatherer instinct tuned to
bands of about 40, which underestimates the inertia of large groups, large strategies and
unseen past negotiations, plus akrasia and a wish for status. Its remedy is a rule of thumb
and group norm: if joining still does net good and the problem is not worth fixing yourself,
join anyway; withhold only when the group blocks a competent person from fixing it, while
conceding that some pressure on a project's leadership is useful.
