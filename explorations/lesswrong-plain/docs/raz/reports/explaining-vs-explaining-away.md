# Report: explaining-vs-explaining-away

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Keats's lines in Lamia lump together three things "cold philosophy" destroyed: haunts in the air, gnomes in the mine, and the rainbow; but the rainbow was explained (it is still there), while the haunts and gnomes were explained away (they never existed). Anti-reductionists miss this distinction, as the classic objection "your belief in reductionism is the mere result of molecules" shows: a causal account of a belief need not debunk it, since the causal chain can run through the fact believed. The confusion comes from the Mind Projection Fallacy: hearing "there are no fundamental rainbows" as "there are no rainbows", and feeling the removal of a belief from one's map as the removal of a thing from the world.
