# Report: my-childhood-role-model

## 1. The argument in three sentences

On the scale of possible minds, the gap between a "village idiot" and Einstein is tiny, since
both have the same brain design, and the right side of the scale belongs to imagined minds
(Jupiter Brains, Deep Thought, the Arisians); Hofstadter's placing Einstein far to the right
shows a cultural gap, which reading science fiction may help close. Human brains are badly
designed for science, so Einstein was a remarkable repurposing of a poor tool and used his
evidence and neurons inefficiently. Hence the author's childhood ideal was a dream of an AI,
not Einstein: ideals made of real, dead humans limit you to what has been done, and the most
important role models are imagined perfections, though an imagined ideal is only your own
mind talking and can lead you astray.
