# Report: raising-the-sanity-waterline ("Raising the Sanity Waterline", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-12)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Religion is a symptom of a general lack of sanity, so eliminating it would leave the larger
failures in place; rationalists should therefore also teach general methods (evidence,
Occam's Razor, avoiding cached thoughts and the author's other topics) that, without ever
mentioning religion, would make religion "go underwater", testable by a five-year follow-up
against a control group. A religious scientist shows that even a basic, one-course
understanding of these methods is not being applied, and religious Nobel laureates (Smalley,
Aumann) show that their colleagues lack it too, since otherwise they would have corrected or
pitied them. So the "sanity waterline" is very low even in science, and the neo-atheists'
victory would leave the real work of rationalists only beginning.
