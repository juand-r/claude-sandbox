# Report: religion-s-claim-to-be-non-disprovable

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Ancient religions made factual claims and presented tests of them (Elijah's contest with the
priests of Baal, the revelation at Sinai, the Exodus, the exorcisms of the New Testament), and
retreated to "you cannot prove or disprove religion" only after the evidence went against
them; religion once claimed authority over law, history, sexual morals, government and
science, and ethics is what is left after better institutions took the rest. But the Bible's
ethics are as obsolete as its science, and people overlook this only because they think they
can get away with it. So the claim that religion is a "separate magisterium" that cannot be
proven or disproven is a "Big Lie", false to history, to scripture, to what children are
taught and to what most believers hold.
