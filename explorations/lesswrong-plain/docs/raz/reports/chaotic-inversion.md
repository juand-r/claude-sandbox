# Report: chaotic-inversion ("Chaotic Inversion", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-11-29)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The author, who struggles to keep working hour by hour, filled unproductive time with short
activities because the return of the ability to work seemed unpredictable. A friend's advice
suggested that other people can predict it, and the author realized that calling one's
work cycle "chaotic" may be the Mind Projection Fallacy: the chaos may be in one's poor
predictions, not in the process. We fail to consider "I'm stupid" not from vanity but
because we do not think of ourselves at all; naming the ignorance does not fix it, but it is
a different path from "it's too chaotic".
