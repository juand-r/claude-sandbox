# Report: no-one-can-exempt-you-from-rationality-s-laws ("No One Can Exempt You From Rationality's Laws", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-10-07)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Traditional Rationality states its rules as social obligations, which makes it natural to answer a demand for evidence with "you can't justify your beliefs either", as if showing the critic to be a hypocrite would excuse you. But the rules of rationality are laws like the second law of thermodynamics: a reliable belief needs evidence as a refrigerator needs energy, and pointing at others who break the rule does not change what happens. No vote or authority can exempt anyone, because the requirement is cause and effect, not a custom.
