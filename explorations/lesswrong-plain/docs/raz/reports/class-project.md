# Report: class-project

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A story, not an argument: Jeffreyssai tells his students they must aspire to do better than
Einstein, whose ten years on General Relativity were "too slow", and sets five of them to
produce the correct theory of quantum gravity in one month. Led by Styrlyn, they write down
angles independently and share them without criticism: avoid Eld science's avenues (Taji),
eliminate infinities (Hiriwa), eliminate time (Yin, from a Lojban inscription), find a
spatially local representation of quantum physics (Styrlyn), and ask why the energy of the
quantum Hamiltonian is necessarily the energy that curves spacetime (Brennan). The story ends
as someone says "I have an idea...", so it shows the method and the stakes, not a result.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- The Weinberg cfact: says physics had "a result of this kind" before 2008 and that it is the
  kind of proof Hiriwa sets aside. I kept it modest ("bears on Brennan's question"). I first
  wrote "from the Lorentz invariance of quantum scattering" and weakened it to "from general
  properties of quantum scattering (the S-matrix)", Porrati's wording, since I saw the
  Lorentz-invariance wording only in search results. A physicist's eye would still help.
- The frame note on the one-month assignment: Eld Science stipulated that the evidence is
  already in hand; the story does not say whether quantum gravity qualifies. It is stated as
  an open point, not as a flaw of the fiction, and is kept out of the "In short" line.
- The Entwurf cfact uses "wrong" for the Entwurf theory; the Einstein's Arrogance notes say
  "That theory was wrong" on the same evidence (18 of 43 arcseconds for Mercury).
- The Response's remark that the book drops two linked posts is neutral (Book III lesson).
