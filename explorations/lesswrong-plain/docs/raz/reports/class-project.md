# Report: class-project

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A story, not an argument: Jeffreyssai tells his students they must aspire to do better than
Einstein, whose ten years on General Relativity were "too slow", and sets five of them to
produce the correct theory of quantum gravity in one month. Led by Styrlyn, they write down
angles independently and share them without criticism: avoid Eld science's avenues (Taji),
eliminate infinities (Hiriwa), eliminate time (Yin, from a Lojban inscription), find a
spatially local representation of quantum physics (Styrlyn), and ask why the energy of the
quantum Hamiltonian is necessarily the energy that curves spacetime (Brennan). The story ends
as someone says "I have an idea...", so it shows the method and the stakes, not a result.
