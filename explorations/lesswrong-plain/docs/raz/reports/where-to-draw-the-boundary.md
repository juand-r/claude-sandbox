# Report: where-to-draw-the-boundary ("Where to Draw the Boundary?", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-02-21)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

A word such as "art" has no meaning floating in the void to be discovered by finding the right
definition; the real task is to find which things cluster together or share a common cause,
that is, to carve reality at its joints ("electromugnetism", which lumps lightning, compasses and
animal magnetism and leaves out light, is a wrong word). Given a list of things called art and
not art, one can propose an intension (the author's: deliberate effort meant to produce
aesthetic emotion), and others can dispute either the intension or the list itself, on the
presumption that some underlying pattern generates the list. Both intensional and extensional
definitions can therefore be wrong, as a "fish" list that includes dolphins is wrong, and one
should admit that one's definition-guesses can be mistaken.
