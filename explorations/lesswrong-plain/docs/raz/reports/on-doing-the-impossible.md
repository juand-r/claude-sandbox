## 1. The argument in three sentences

Looking back on work in AI, the author finds that problems first dismissed as "impossible"
(understanding intelligence precisely, Friendly AI) came to look merely very difficult after
years of work, because "impossible" usually means "I can't see any way to do that," a fact
about a confused map, not a proof about the territory. Since a problem that seems impossible
will not be tried, and only trying for years lets the confusion clear, perseverance has parts
at three timescales: not running away in the first seconds, working through the hours, and
sticking with the problem for years. The author warns that attempting the impossible is for
very special occasions only, since talent is merely the ante and the stake is years of your
life, which you can lose.
