# Report: rationality-common-interest-of-many-causes ("Rationality: Common Interest of Many Causes", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-29)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Many causes (atheism, marijuana legalization, the author's own institute) benefit when people become more rational, so they could share common material on rationality and accept that they will not capture all the value they create while capturing some of what others create. Because people are "altruistic akrasics" rather than expected-utility maximizers, publicly arguing over which cause is best raises the burden of proof, invites motivation-sapping comparisons and may shrink the total supply of altruism; giving is not a fixed pie of fungible dollars. So causes should say "here is a cool project" in general forums, keep disputes over top priority to specialized venues, exclude projects that are not real tasks for humanity, and handle free riders by dropping them from shared resources.
