# Report: a-rational-argument

## 1. The argument in three sentences

An argument whose conclusion was fixed in advance cannot be a rational argument, even if
every fact in it is true and relevant, because the selection of facts (here, a campaign
dropping the one unfavourable answer from a questionnaire) forces readers to infer hidden
evidence. An argument can be valid or invalid regardless of the truth of its premises and
conclusion, and a "hypocritical" argument is one whose stated reasons are not its real
reasons. The only honest argument for a candidate is the checklist you used to choose that
candidate before anyone hired you, because only what actually decided the bottom line can
honestly be written above it.
