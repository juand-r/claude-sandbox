# Report: resist-the-happy-death-spiral

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Some ideas really are that good (Bacon's scientific method is the example), so the cure for
a happy death spiral cannot be refusing to admire anything, nor a biased hunt for negatives,
nor confining the idea to a narrow box, since science does bear on nearly everything. The
cure is to treat each additional nice claim as a burdensome detail and ask for the specific
causal chain behind it, so that the halo stays subcritical; claims that are merely
"unproven" should not add to one's happiness. Stuart Armstrong's advice, to split the Great
Idea into independent parts and judge each separately, serves the same end, and the post
closes with a list of what does and does not work.
