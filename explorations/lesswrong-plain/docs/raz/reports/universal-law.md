# Report: universal-law

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Lavoisier and, before him, Newton unified realms humans had kept apart, and Newton's work
gave rise to the idea of a universal law with literally no exceptions, an idea that seems
fanatical to minds used to leaky everyday categories and to moral rules that allow
exceptions. But the universe is, by human standards, "insanely rigid": when a model fails,
the replacement is again exceptionless, and the universe was always obeying it; and a
probability that a law is exceptionless is not a law that holds only part of the time.
The ancient Greeks, who thought contrived experiments gave "monstrous" results, missed
this; the lesson, put as a verse, is that "not one unusual thing has ever happened."

## 5. Not verified

- The verse's origin (see above): "could not find ... earlier", not "invented".
- The post's claim about Greek philosophers' view of "monstrous" results: I found no source
  for or against the specific attitude; the note only gives Galen as a case that sits
  uneasily with "didn't realize you could generalize from experiments".
- Conservation of momentum "from the uttermost dawn of time": in general relativity only
  local conservation holds in an expanding universe. Hedged by the post ("As far as we
  know") and a nitpick for its argument; not noted.

## 6. Judgment calls

- The Descartes cfact: credit as information. The post says Newton's discovery "gave rise
  to the notion"; the note says the notion is older. A fair defender might say the post
  meant that Newton's success made the notion credible; the note's last sentence grants
  that ("gave the idea its most striking example").
- The Galen note uses "sits uneasily with", not "contradicts": Galen was a Greek physician
  under the Roman Empire, and the post's claim is hedged as "One of the reasons".
- I cut a draft note on the "customary accusation made against scientists" (no accuser
  named): judged "any essay does that" (STANDARDS 2.4(4)).
- Reserved-word flags: "never" appears only in paraphrase of the post's hedged "not one
  single violation" with the post's "As far as we know" kept.
- raz_check flags the honest section's quotation of the verse ("Since the beginning / not
  one unusual thing / has ever happened."): the slashes mark the original's line breaks;
  the words match the original.
