# Report: why-our-kind-can-t-cooperate ("Why Our Kind Can't Cooperate", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-20)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Starting from an online fundraising appeal the author ran, in which people who declined to
give posted their objections to the mailing lists while donors stayed silent, the post argues
that the "atheist/libertarian/technophile" crowd coordinates worse than a synagogue or a
flying-saucer cult, and that a true rationalist should be effective and so should not accept
that the "Dark Side" must win by better coordination. Its diagnosis is half-rationality: a
culture that practices and rewards only disagreement (the conference Q&A where praise would be
horrifying), and a shame of strong feeling (the Spock archetype), whereas knowing more should
never make a group do worse. The remedy is to train both sides, agreement as well as dissent,
to allow emotion that fits the facts, and to voice support publicly as well as objections, as
the synagogue's members did with their pledges.
