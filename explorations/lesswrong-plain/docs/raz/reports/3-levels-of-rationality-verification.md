# Report: 3-levels-of-rationality-verification ("3 Levels of Rationality Verification", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-15)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The author strongly suspects that a stronger art of rationality is possible, and says that
what a group can do about it depends overwhelmingly on methods for verifying which ideas
work. Verification comes at three levels, illustrated from martial arts, happiness research,
the SAT, degree-granting colleges and hedge funds: reputational (masters test themselves in
realistic contests), experimental (standard measurements comparing randomly assigned
methods, where noise can be averaged away) and organizational (tests of individuals that
resist gaming, since a gameable test makes a field lose its purpose). The post proposes no
measures itself; it asks readers to brainstorm, even stupid ideas, for all three levels.
