# Report: that-tiny-note-of-discord

Batch 6a (Book VI, Yudkowsky's Coming of Age, order 304). Posted 2008-09-23.

## 1. The argument in three sentences

In 2000 the young author, still holding the 1997 view that building a superintelligence is right
if anything is, noticed that people might want an AI not to kill them even if life is
meaningless, which would create a present conflict of interest over the Singularity; instead of
taking the "obvious dodge", perfectionism, a wish not to wrong the funder Brian Atkins, and a rule
of honesty led the young author to ask how to specify a fallback morality for an AI. This was the
first attempt to think technically about putting a morality into an AI without "the escape-hatch
of the mysterious essence of rightness", and the practice began to make morality less
mysterious. The lesson is that actions screen off justifications: what mattered was working out
the details, not the reasons for doing so; AI builders with clever reasons not to think about
Friendly AI should look at their actions stripped of justifications, and a world-changing truth
may first appear as one small, easily dismissed thought.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- The SL4/CFAI note and the Response paragraph built on it ("One step of the story was told
  differently at the time"; "the account of what came first is not the one given in 2001"). The
  two accounts may be compatible (the note and Response say so). I think the reader should know
  the contemporaneous account; the editor may prefer to keep it in the note only and out of the
  In short line.
- The "unnamed builders" point (note on the excuses paragraph, Response, In short). The post puts
  the excuses in quotation marks as typical sayings; I say "No one is named", not that they are
  misquoted. The motive phrase ("just basically aren't interested") is quoted, not inferred.
- The Shermer credit: the post puts the phrase in quotation marks without attribution; the
  wording differs ("stupid", "unskilled reasons"). I call it an adaptation, not a misquotation.
- The Exodus note says the post's reading is "closest to the ethical one" (Rashbam); that is my
  comparison, from Wikipedia's summary.
- "Reasons don't matter" versus "perfectionism really matters": treated as information with the
  next post's reconciliation, not as a contradiction (the post itself grants "there's a lesson in
  his being a perfectionist").
- Pronouns: the post calls Eliezer2000 "he"; I avoided pronouns for the young author in notes,
  and the honest section uses "I" for the young self's thoughts and actions.
- "invented" (raz_check flag) describes the post's lie detectors, and "wrong" is a verb ("not to
  wrong Brian Atkins"); neither is a verdict.
