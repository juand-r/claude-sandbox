# Report: that-tiny-note-of-discord

Batch 6a (Book VI, Yudkowsky's Coming of Age, order 304). Posted 2008-09-23.

## 1. The argument in three sentences

In 2000 the young author, still holding the 1997 view that building a superintelligence is right
if anything is, noticed that people might want an AI not to kill them even if life is
meaningless, which would create a present conflict of interest over the Singularity; instead of
taking the "obvious dodge", perfectionism, a wish not to wrong the funder Brian Atkins, and a rule
of honesty led the young author to ask how to specify a fallback morality for an AI. This was the
first attempt to think technically about putting a morality into an AI without "the escape-hatch
of the mysterious essence of rightness", and the practice began to make morality less
mysterious. The lesson is that actions screen off justifications: what mattered was working out
the details, not the reasons for doing so; AI builders with clever reasons not to think about
Friendly AI should look at their actions stripped of justifications, and a world-changing truth
may first appear as one small, easily dismissed thought.
