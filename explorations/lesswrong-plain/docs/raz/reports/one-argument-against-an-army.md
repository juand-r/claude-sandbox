# Report: one-argument-against-an-army

## 1. The argument in three sentences

When people meet a new counterargument, they avoid lowering their confidence by recalling the
supporting arguments they already knew, and so each new objection seems to be defeated "three
to one" and their confidence even grows. This is double-counting: the old arguments were
already included in the old confidence, and counting only the supporting ones again is worse
than counting everything twice. Even when the balance still favours a belief, each piece of
contrary evidence must move the probability down.
