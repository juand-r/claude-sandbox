# Report: the-futility-of-emergence

## 1. The argument in three sentences

"Emergence", as commonly used ("Intelligence is an emergent phenomenon!"), is a present-day
mysterious answer like phlogiston and vitalism: defined as high-level behaviour arising from
many low-level interactions, it fits every phenomenon above quarks and so explains nothing.
The verb "emerges from" is acceptable when a specific model follows, but the noun used as an
explanation meets every sign of a mysterious answer, as two exercises show: deleting
"emergent" from a sentence changes nothing, and replacing it with "magical" conveys the same
knowledge. The word is popular because it is "the junk food of curiosity", and people with
some science education use it the way their ancestors used magic.
