# Report: the-genetic-fallacy ("The Genetic Fallacy", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-07-11)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The genetic fallacy, attacking a belief because of its causes, is formally a fallacy because a belief's original cause is not its current justification, but since people change their minds less often than they think, where a belief came from matters more for humans than for ideal Bayesians. When a source you relied on is discredited (the Bible is the archetype), you should clear your mind and be very suspicious of everything that grew from it, without simply reversing it, unless clear-cut evidence, as for Kekulé's benzene ring, has made the source irrelevant. The genetic fallacy is a fallacy only when other justifications exist but the genetic accusation is presented as settling the issue; the post ends with four rules of thumb for when to attend to origins and when to prefer technical arguments.
