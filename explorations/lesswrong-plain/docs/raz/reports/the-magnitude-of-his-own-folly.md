# Report: The Magnitude of His Own Folly (order 308)

Agent: batch 6a. Slug: `the-magnitude-of-his-own-folly`. Posted 2008-09-30.

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The author recounts the moment, dated to late 2002, of seeing what the 1997 AI proposals
would really have done, and the two reactions the author avoided: the proud "I almost
destroyed the world" and the minimizing "it wasn't really loaded." The lesson was to stop and
say "I'm not ready," which the AGI field punishes with lost status; the deeper reason the
author had not stopped earlier was not believing, at gut level, that "Nature was allowed to
kill me", and the author guesses that other AGI researchers are in the same state. What the
author then understood is that no one is graded on a curve, that good arguments and good
conduct do not prevent the consequences of a risk, and that the only safe course in
ignorance was not to proceed until the ground was known to be safe; this, the post says, is
when the author's training as a rationalist began.
