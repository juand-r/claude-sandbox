# Report: fake-optimization-criteria

## 1. The argument in three sentences

People can argue that almost any goal implies almost any policy, and since human motives
and the world are complicated there is rarely a case where we know exactly what a stated
criterion recommends, against which to measure the rationalizations. Natural selection is
such a case: it optimizes only for inclusive genetic fitness, so the group-selection story
lets us compare the group selectionists' prediction (restraint) with the result of pure
optimization for a known criterion (cannibalism). We should practise on such simple cases
before trying to see what complex human moral criteria really recommend.
