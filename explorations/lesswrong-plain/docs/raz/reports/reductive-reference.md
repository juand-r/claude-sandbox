# Report: reductive-reference ("Reductive Reference", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-04-03; book order 224)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Human minds use a multi-level map (hands and fingers, heat and kinetic energy) while reality
seems single-level (quarks), so the post asks in what sense a belief such as "snow is white"
can be true, taking truth in Tarski's sense as a comparison between belief and reality that
experiments, not introspection, test. Its answer is that a word like "snow" is a
"promissory note": an empirically determined pointer to whatever simple boundary around the
unseen causes of our experience an ideal omniscient interpreter would draw, as in Putnam's
Twin Earth, and the same holds for our beliefs about our own beliefs, which the post calls
self-consistent rather than circular. So "there are no fundamental hands" is not "there are
no hands": higher-level things exist implicitly in the single level of physics, and the map
can be true without specifying any quark positions.

## 6. Judgment calls

1. The Twin Earth cpara and the Response call the promissory note "close to Putnam's own
   conclusion" and say the post "does not say that its own view is the conclusion Putnam drew".
   The post does credit Twin Earth and "the subsequent philosophical debate", so this is
   information, not a charge of taking credit. The "In short" line says "credited only through
   Twin Earth"; check that this is fair.
2. The postmodernist cpara: "No postmodernist is named or quoted" plus the observation that the
   harder form of the question is deferred by the post itself. Not a motive reading.
3. The Tarski cpara makes a pointer, not a new criticism (2.5).
4. The self-consistency cpara: I dropped an earlier draft that said consistency is too weak a
   property (a defender can reply that "reflective coherence" is the reason given); the note now
   only credits precedents and points to the later post.
5. Almost all notes are descriptive; the post gives little ground for fair criticism beyond the
   points above. The Response is correspondingly mild.
