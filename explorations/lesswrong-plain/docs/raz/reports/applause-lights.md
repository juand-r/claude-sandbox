# Report: applause-lights

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A conference speaker called for "democratic" development of AI, and when questioned could not
say how conflicts between interest groups would be resolved, which the author takes to show
that the word was used to make the audience cheer, like the "Applause" sign in a TV studio,
rather than to propose anything. Since the substance of democracy is a specific
conflict-resolution mechanism, calling for democracy without one conveys nothing; many such
"applause lights" can be detected by reversing the sentence and seeing that the reversal
sounds abnormal. Uninformative sentences have legitimate uses, but if no specifics follow,
the sentence is probably an applause light, and a talk made only of them would be absurd.
