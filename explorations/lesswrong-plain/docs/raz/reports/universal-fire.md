# Report: universal-fire

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A fantasy story in which a match fails to strike in a world of magic is impossible in a
deeper sense than it seems: Lavoisier showed that fire is combustion, and that breathing is
the same process, so the rules "matches light" and "humans breathe" are joined in nature.
Matches work because of phosphorus, and phosphorus also underlies ATP, the body's energy
carrier, and phosphorus's behavior follows from the laws of electrons and quarks, so a
world where matches fail would be a world where you could not exist. Our minds store
surface rules in separate compartments, but the territory is not divided that way: reality
is "laced together" more tightly than we like to believe.
