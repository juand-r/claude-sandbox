# Report: your-rationality-is-my-business

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Commenters asked what business the author has criticizing others' choices, such as
buying lottery tickets or believing what is pleasant; the author answers that it is
right to have an interest in humanity's future, which includes the pursuit of truth, so
other people's rationality is the author's business. This is dangerous, since people
have been burned for thinking wrongly, but the remedy is to deny the "therefore": argue
against bad ideas, never use violence or regulation against their holders, as science
settles disputes by experiment and not by force. Relativists and advocates of
selfishness are not really neutral, since they argue for their side; and though private
self-deception does little harm, the author cannot help caring how others think.
