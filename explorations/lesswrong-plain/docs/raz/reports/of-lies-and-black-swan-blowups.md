# Report: of-lies-and-black-swan-blowups ("Of Lies and Black Swan Blowups", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-04-07)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Marcus Einfeld, a decorated Australian judge, went to jail for perjury and lies that began with a $77 speeding ticket. This is a real example of the theory (from "Entangled Truths, Contagious Lies") that lies leave traces and can blow up catastrophically. The author grants that the theory sounds suspiciously virtuous and that selective reporting explains why we hear of this case.
