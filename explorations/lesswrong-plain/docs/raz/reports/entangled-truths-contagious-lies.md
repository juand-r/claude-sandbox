# Report: entangled-truths-contagious-lies ("Entangled Truths, Contagious Lies", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-10-15)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Everything is physically connected to its past, but only some connections are strong enough to notice, and these form a network (the "Great Web of Causality") in which a pebble or a fact carries marks of its origin that an expert could read. A lie is a fact out of place in this network, so defending it can require further lies about connected facts, and humans, not being gods, often fail to foresee which facts will expose them. Not all lies are caught, but lies are riskier than liars think, so honesty or silence carries less unseen risk than lying.
