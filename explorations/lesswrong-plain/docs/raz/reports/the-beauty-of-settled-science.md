# Report: the-beauty-of-settled-science

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The media report only the frontier of science, where evidence is thinnest and findings are
"wrong half the time," and controversies are, for outsiders, only the fun of picking a
side. Textbooks give careful explanations, derivations, exercises and a reasonable
guarantee of truth, while press releases give fake explanations of results that probably
will not replicate. So one should take joy in settled science, starting at the beginning
with elementary textbooks, because science need not be controversial or recent to be
interesting, and jumping to the frontier without the basics does not work.
