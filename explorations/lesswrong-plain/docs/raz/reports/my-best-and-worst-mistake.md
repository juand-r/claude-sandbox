# Report: my-best-and-worst-mistake

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Having seen definitions of intelligence abused in AI, and simple elegant math confine AI to
toy systems, the young Yudkowsky refused to define intelligence and disbelieved in any
mathematics of it, overreacting to others' mistakes. This was a best mistake because it led
to broad study of the cognitive sciences and a long period of study before proposing
anything, and the author draws the moral that what you end up doing matters more than the
clever reason you had for doing it. It was a worst mistake because informality meant low
standards: reasoning at the level of "that orangey-bright thing" is needed on the way to
knowledge, but must not be trusted, and the young author put weight on it and "stepped onto
a loaded trap".
