# Report: think-like-reality ("Think Like Reality", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-05-02)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Calling quantum physics (or any well-checked fact) "weird" puts the fault in reality, when
the fault is in intuitions that evolution shaped for medium-sized objects and that are
simply poor models of the fundamental level. Since surprise measures a poor model, the
right response to persistent surprise is to reshape one's intuitions until reality looks
normal, without pretending to a comfort one lacks, and without wasting effort on
indignation. The same holds outside physics ("I just don't understand how..."): surprise is
in the map, not the territory, so once a fact checks out, the problem is the model.
