# Report: belief-in-self-deception

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Continuing the previous day's conversation, the author reports that the woman could not
answer a counterfactual about a world without God, offered "I believe that people are nicer
than they really are" as a counterexample to the Litany of Tarski, and said that nothing
should be done differently if there were no God. The author concludes that her religion is
the worship of her own attempt at self-deception: the attempt failed, but because she
believes it succeeded she gets a placebo benefit, and this makes the "worship of worship"
a new target for those defending sanity. This explains why the author argues that
self-deception is hard: saying so removes the placebo benefit of believing one has deceived
oneself.
