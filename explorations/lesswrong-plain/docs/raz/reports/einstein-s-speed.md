# Report: Einstein's Speed (batch 4d, order 256)

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Individual scientists sometimes rationally know things before Science confirms them, and not
only by spotting regularities in data: Einstein reached General Relativity by armchair
reasoning from principles (relativity of motion, equivalence of inertial and gravitational
mass), using Mercury's perihelion only as a final check. In Bayesian terms this is still
induction, with a simplicity prior over the possible "characters of physical law", updated on
the other known laws, so his confidence before the 1919 eclipse (and against Bohr) was
justified. The feat is possible but hard and rare, history over-remembers the winners, and
ordinary people should use every scrap of evidence, though they may train themselves to
reason from scanty evidence where they will later learn whether they were right.
