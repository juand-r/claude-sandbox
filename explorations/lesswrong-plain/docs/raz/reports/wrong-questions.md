## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Some questions cannot be answered on their own terms and must be dissolved by understanding
the mental algorithm that makes them feel like questions; a good cue is that no imaginable
state of the world would answer them (the falling tree, free will, why anything exists).
Mystery and confusion are in the map, not the territory, and trying to answer such questions
produces mysterious answers that predict nothing. In the author's experience such questions
are always solvable, and a question that seems absolutely impossible signals confusion.
