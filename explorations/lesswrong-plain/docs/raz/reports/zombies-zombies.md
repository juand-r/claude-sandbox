# Report: zombies-zombies

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The zombie argument says that a world physically identical to ours but without consciousness
is possible, so consciousness is something over and above the physical; the post argues that
seeing no contradiction in that world is not the same as its being logically possible, and
that the world is not even intuitive once you notice that our talk about consciousness seems
to be caused by consciousness, while the zombie twins would say and write the same things.
Chalmers accepts this consequence (the "paradox of phenomenal judgment") and answers that his
beliefs are justified by his having the experiences, but the post replies that the causally
closed physical part of Chalmers, which writes his papers, would then be producing beliefs that
are right only by a separate, unexplained coincidence, which a self-inspecting reasoner (or AI)
should treat as a malfunction. So epiphenomenal property dualism posits more unexplained
"miracles" than either interactionist substance dualism or a reductionism that has not yet
explained consciousness, and the sane response to the zombie argument is to say it cannot be
right and look for the flaw.
