# Report: just-lose-hope-already ("Just Lose Hope Already", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-02-25)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Casey Serin ran up $2.2 million of debt on eight houses bought with fraudulent mortgage applications and still refuses to give up, and Long-Term Capital Management, whose partners included the Nobel laureates Merton and Scholes, kept borrowing more to exploit shrinking margins after its edge had gone. Both show what happens when you refuse to lose hope. Although each profession has its own skills, not turning little mistakes into big ones seems to the author to be a common art, and one of its keys is to be ready to admit you lost.
