# Report: when-none-dare-urge-restraint ("When None Dare Urge Restraint", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-12-08)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

On the morning of 11 September 2001 the author predicted that the overreaction would be ten times worse than the attack, because everyone would compete to express horror and no one would dare to urge a proportionate response. This is the "spiral of hate", the mirror of the happy death spiral: attacking the Enemy earns credit and correcting any negative claim about the Enemy counts as treason, so even false claims (the hijackers were "cowards") go unchallenged. The US response did more damage to the US than the terrorists could, ignoring the attack would have been better, and once restraint became unspeakable, within two days, fury and folly could only rise.
