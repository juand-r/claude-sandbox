# Report: the-wonder-of-evolution

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Evolution ends the regress of designers not by being clever but by being stupid and
inefficient and working anyway, and that is the thing to marvel at. The creationist picture
of parts shaken together in a box misdescribes evolution, which builds complex machinery in
small steps or by reusing old machinery (the RNA world is the worked example), and which
does not claim to explain the first replicator, a crude accident. Praising evolution beyond
what science supports is inaccuracy, not loyalty to science, and evolution is not a designer
humans should imitate (Huxley).
