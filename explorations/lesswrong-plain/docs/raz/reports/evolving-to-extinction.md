# Report: evolving-to-extinction

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Natural selection changes gene frequencies, and a gene spreads according to how it helps its
own copies, not how it helps the species; the sex ratio shows this, since individual
selection keeps it near equal investment where group selection would favour more females.
So genes that harm or even doom their species can spread: a self-sacrificing "Frodo gene"
vanishes, and segregation distorters, transposons, a perfect DNA-copying mechanism,
over-replicating viruses, cancer cells and perhaps a bystander effect show how an
evolving population can drive itself extinct. Evolution shows no concern for individuals,
species or even genes, and the author suggests humanity may be finishing this process now.

## 3. Arithmetic

- Group A (2M+2F per mother): children 4; daughters 2, so grandchildren 2 x 4 = 8; of these
  4 are daughters, so great-grandchildren 4 x 4 = 16. Group B (3F+1M): children 4; 3
  daughters, grandchildren 12; 9 of them daughters, great-grandchildren 36. 12/8 = 1.5 (50%
  more); 36/16 = 2.25 (125% more). Correct, assuming males are never limiting.
- Ten-to-one ratio: each child has one father and one mother, so with 1 male per 10 females a
  male has on average 10 times as many offspring as a female. The post says "grandchildren";
  from the deviant mother's side these are her grandchildren. No note.
- Equal investment: if sons cost half as much, the evolutionarily stable ratio is 2 sons per
  daughter (equal total expenditure). The post's statement is correct.
- Driving Y: a normal male passes Y to half his offspring, a driver male to all (post says all
  offspring are male carriers), so twice the copies for the same number of offspring. Correct.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- The cfact on the mouse segregation distorter is the strongest note. It says "I could not
  find such a mouse gene", and describes the t haplotype, the case group selection was in fact
  invoked for. My reading, stated as "The mouse example is another matter", is that the post
  merged the t-haplotype story (low frequency, group selection) with Hamilton's driving-Y
  model. I did not write "conflates" or "false".

  guesses and one observed case (cancer). The post claims possibility, which its thought

- Several notes give credit or information (Williams, Fisher, local mate competition,
  evolutionary suicide, maize 85%). They are marked as information where they could be read as

  ya"), but it is a factual claim used to support the conclusion about humanity, so I kept it.
