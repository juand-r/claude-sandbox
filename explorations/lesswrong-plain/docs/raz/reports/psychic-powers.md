# Report: psychic-powers

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The author concedes Benja Fallenstein's objection to the previous post: even if every test we
can devise could be passed by a reducible world, an outcome can still make irreducibility much
more probable under a simplicity prior, and the author adds a second flaw of the same kind.
If beliefs were ontologically basic, a direct law linking minds would be simple, so psychic
powers are a privileged probabilistic prediction of non-reductionist (supernatural, in Carrier's
sense) worldviews, while for reductionists, whose minds are complex constructs of neurons, they
are not expected. So discovering psychic powers would be strong Bayesian evidence for
non-reductionism, and, the author concludes, that is why the author is confident in dismissing
them as a priori implausible despite the claimed experimental evidence.
