# Report: explain-worship-ignore

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Faced with something you cannot explain, such as rain, you can ignore the question, try to
explain it, or enjoy its mysteriousness; the post calls these Ignore, Explain and Worship.
Each explanation raises a new question, from life to chemistry to atoms to quarks to the Big
Bang, and the tempting way out, a final "Explanation That Needs No Explanation", is itself
Worship. The alternative is to treat the regress as a puzzle ("I wonder how the apparent
paradox is resolved?"), and to choose among the three options wisely.

   a charge against the author). Pronoun "he" in the Summary refers to the wise man, a
   character, as the post uses "He".
2. The "There—I just hit Worship" note says the post "does not say which part earns the
   label" (the claim that the chain ends, or the reverence). A fair defender could reply
   that the Explain alternative two paragraphs later shows the difference (treating the
   regress as an open question). I kept the note because that alternative contrasts
   attitudes and still does not say whether concluding that the chain ends is Worship.
   Consider softening if the editor reads the post as clear on this.
3. The "sky spirits" clogic (Explain covers made-up answers) could be read as faulting a
   fable for its example. I kept it because the post calls sky spirits the "best-case
   scenario" of Explain; the note says only what the categories do and do not sort.
4. The Response says "Much of the post restates the two posts of the week before." This is
   a structural judgment; the matched passages are in section 4.
5. The last cpara says the post "has said nothing about when setting a question aside is
   the wise option." I read the whole post for such a criterion: the Ignore paragraph lists
   cases (unimportant, irrelevant, put off) but gives no test of when that is wise.
