# Report: Trying to Try (order 311)

Agent: batch 6b. Slug: `trying-to-try`. Posted 2008-10-01.

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Saying "I will flip the switch" already means making and carrying out a plan that gives the
best chance of flipping it, so saying "I will try to flip it" moves the goal from the switch
to having a plan that might flip it. For humans this lower goal is easy to meet by
self-deception: almost any effort, the first plan that comes to mind, and whatever resources
are convenient can be counted as "trying our best". So one should aim at the outcome itself,
asking what needs to be done rather than what one can do: "Don't try your best. Win, or fail."
