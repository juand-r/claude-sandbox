# Report: helpless-individuals ("Helpless Individuals", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-30)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Human grouping instincts fit 50-person bands, so the large institutions that exist are the few kinds that do not depend on individuals voluntarily serving a common interest: governments that tax, corporations with central control of money, virulent religions, and powerful individuals; most large institutions that would serve collective individual interests "fail to exist in the first place." Science is the example: individuals rarely fund it because it is a poor emotional fit for personal giving (long horizons, no volunteering, no immediate feedback), so it is funded by governments, corporations and foundations, and scientists then compete for pre-allocated money. Science thus survives as a "parasite" on the few large organizations that can exist, while many other projects never come into being.
