# Report: tolerate-tolerance ("Tolerate Tolerance", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-21)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

People who set out to be rationalists probably have a higher-than-usual annoyance at flawed
reasoning, so if they want to work together they must tolerate others' tolerance, as the
author has learned to with Ben Goertzel, who compliments almost everyone. Punishing someone
for their own mistakes is one thing, but punishing them for refusing to castigate others
(punishment of non-punishers) can lock in an equilibrium harmful to everyone, so the author
resolves to judge people only for their own "un-borrowed" mistakes. A group that demands you
hate the right enemies may be the wrong group, but you should not demand that your friends be
equally intolerant of that group either.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- The clogic note (and Response paragraph, and n.b.): the Goertzel example "mixes" refusing to condemn with praising an idea, and the post "does not separate" discounting praise as evidence from punishing. This is my analysis of the post's own categories ("mistakes they make themselves, directly" versus "refusing to castigate"; "own un-borrowed mistakes"). A fair defender could say the compliment was social kindness, not an endorsement; the FAQ's report that Goertzel later said he had no definite opinion fits either reading. It appears in the In short line; if the editor judges it contestable, it could move to the notes only.
- The Popper note says "``Inconsistently'' is asserted without argument", which is true of the post. It does not claim Popper refutes the post. "contradiction" appears once in the Response, in "not a contradiction" describing Popper's view.
- Considered and dropped: "Cooperation is unstable ... without some kind of punishment" is broader than the literature (kin selection sustains cooperation without punishment); it does not affect the argument, so nitpick. The possibility that the post's closing advice (leave a group that punishes non-punishers) is itself a limited intolerance of intolerance: the post answers it by judging Group X for its own conduct, so dropped.
- Pronouns: raz_check flags "he/him/his" for Ben Goertzel. Goertzel is a person in the post, which uses "him" and "he" ("pays off for him", "work with him"); STANDARDS 2.6 lets characters keep the post's pronouns. The post's author gets none.
