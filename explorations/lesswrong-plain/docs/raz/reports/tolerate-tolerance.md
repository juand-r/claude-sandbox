# Report: tolerate-tolerance ("Tolerate Tolerance", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-21)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

People who set out to be rationalists probably have a higher-than-usual annoyance at flawed
reasoning, so if they want to work together they must tolerate others' tolerance, as the
author has learned to with Ben Goertzel, who compliments almost everyone. Punishing someone
for their own mistakes is one thing, but punishing them for refusing to castigate others
(punishment of non-punishers) can lock in an equilibrium harmful to everyone, so the author
resolves to judge people only for their own "un-borrowed" mistakes. A group that demands you
hate the right enemies may be the wrong group, but you should not demand that your friends be
equally intolerant of that group either.
