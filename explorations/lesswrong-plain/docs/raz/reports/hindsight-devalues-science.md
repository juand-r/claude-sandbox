# Report: hindsight-devalues-science

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Critics say social science only finds what anyone would expect, but that expectation is
hindsight: shown five reversed findings about World War II soldiers, each with a plausible
explanation, readers find them predictable, and students rate both a finding and its opposite
as what they would have predicted. So hindsight makes scientific findings look less surprising
than they are, especially in fields we understand, which undervalues the researchers and keeps
us from noticing evidence that does not fit what we really expected. We must make a conscious
effort to be shocked enough.
