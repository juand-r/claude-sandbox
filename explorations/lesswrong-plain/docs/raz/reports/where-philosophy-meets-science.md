## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The most basic error of early quantum physics was that its founders forgot they were
themselves made of particles, so they did not see that a detector (or their own knowledge)
is just more "particles in different places", and they brought consciousness into a theory
that could not be written as a formal program. The insight they needed looks
"philosophical", yet it was physicists, not professional philosophers, who cleared up the
mystery, and in general science, not the consensus of philosophers, tells us which
philosopher was right. So "philosophy" is the name for thinking not yet taught or reduced to
calculation, and it is done well only from within a science by people closely involved with
it; professional philosophers should learn the science and expect experiment to settle their
arguments.
