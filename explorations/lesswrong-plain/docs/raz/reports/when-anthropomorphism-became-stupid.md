# Report: when-anthropomorphism-became-stupid ("When Anthropomorphism Became Stupid", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-08-16; book order 222)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Animism, the belief that trees, rivers and rocks have minds, was not an obviously stupid
mistake for earlier cultures: people reasoned by analogy from their own bodies and wills, and
nothing they could see ruled it out. It became obviously wrong only gradually, once people
knew that thought is done by the brain's tangled neural circuitry, that complex functional
structure comes from natural selection, and that cognition is computation. With that
knowledge one can look at a tree, a river, an atom, a puppy or a rock and give a reason why
it lacks a mind (or moral reasoning), which is why the post ends "Now we know" after the
Zhuangzi story of the happy fish.
