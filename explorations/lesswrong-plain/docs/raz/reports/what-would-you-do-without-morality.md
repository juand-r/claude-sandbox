# Report: what-would-you-do-without-morality ("What Would You Do Without Morality?", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-06-29; book order 276)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The post asks the reader to suppose it were proved that nothing is moral or right, that all
utilities equal zero, and to imagine concretely what they would then do. It lists questions
(tipping, fidelity, a child on the tracks, food, gloomy poetry, getting out of bed, reading,
rationality) and heads off the stock nihilist answer as a cached thought. It gives no
answers: the point, left implicit, is that the reader will find most choices unchanged.
