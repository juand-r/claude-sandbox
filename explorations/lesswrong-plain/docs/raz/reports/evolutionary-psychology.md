# Report: evolutionary-psychology ("Evolutionary Psychology", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-11-11)

Book order 141. Batch 3a.

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Every organ, including the brain's circuits for lust and anger, exists because it contributed to
ancestors' reproduction, so anger exists because angry ancestors had more children. That
historical fact is not a fact about anyone's present motives: cognitive causes (made of neurons)
and evolutionary causes (made of ancestors) are different things, and a human is harder to think
about than a toaster only because a human is also a mind with purposes of its own. So one must
keep apart the episode of anger, the circuitry, the genes and the ancestral history, as the story
of two strangers in a bar, each driven by ancestral cues with no wish for children, shows.
