# Report: einstein-s-superpowers

## 1. The argument in three sentences

People treat Einstein as having magical, sacred potential, so that both the crackpot who
compares himself to Einstein and the critic who is scandalized by a young physicist's hope to
do better share one error: confusing Einstein's social status with research potential. What
separated Einstein from the many bright people dismissed as "just another Jewish genius" was,
the author suspects, not innate brilliance but choosing an important problem, finding a
worthwhile angle of attack, and persisting for years; before his papers he would have shown
no "aura of destiny". The author, who says they have such a problem and meet people demanding
to see their aura, credits Julian Barbour with seeing through Einstein, and concludes that
one acquires such powers by seeing that they are perfectly normal.
