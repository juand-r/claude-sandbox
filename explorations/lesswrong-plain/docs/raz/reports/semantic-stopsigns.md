# Report: semantic-stopsigns

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Asked where the universe came from, some people answer "God!", although "Where did God
come from?" follows at once; the author, following Jonathan Wallace, explains this by saying
that "God!" works as a "semantic stopsign", a word that signals "do not think past this
point" rather than a claim. Other words do the same for other people ("Liberal democracy!"
for an acquaintance, "Corporations!" for some), and the test is whether a person asks of
their favourite word the obvious next questions they would ask of any other. No word is a
stopsign in itself; what makes one is a particular person's "failure to consider the obvious
next question".
