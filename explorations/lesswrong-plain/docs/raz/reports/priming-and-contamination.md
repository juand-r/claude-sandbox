# Report: priming-and-contamination

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Seeing one word speeds recognition of related words, an automatic effect of spreading
activation, and anchors work partly the same way: comparing a temperature to 5°C or 20°C
speeds recognition of cold or hot words. More generally, uninformative or irrelevant
information "contaminates" estimates and decisions, and the author says modern research
attributes most anchoring to this rather than to adjustment. Because any idea primes
compatible thoughts, contamination is a form of confirmation bias built into the brain, so
one quick flash can already decide the conclusion.
