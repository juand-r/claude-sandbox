# Report: 37-ways-that-words-can-be-wrong

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Definitions and categories can be "wrong" in the sense that matters in practice, because
the brain categorizes and infers without supervision, so saying "I can define a word any
way I like" is nearly always an error. The post lists 37 such errors, one or more for each
earlier post of the sequence, with an example and a link for each: words that connect to
nothing, empirical claims made "by definition", arguments about definitions, dictionaries
used as authorities, connotations sneaked in, boundaries drawn where things do not cluster,
and words whose meaning shifts. It ends by comparing "Words are arbitrary; I can define a
word any way I like" to steering a car over thin ice at random.
