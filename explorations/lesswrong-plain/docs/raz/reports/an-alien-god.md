# Report: an-alien-god

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Nature is full of apparent purpose, but its purposes conflict (foxes and rabbits) and are
often cruel (ichneumon wasps, starving elephants), which fits one benevolent designer
poorly; evolution explains both because there is no "Evolution Fairy" deciding what is
helpful, only genes whose effects cause their own copies to become more frequent, separately
in each population and without foresight. Hence people who imagine what would be good for
organisms predict wrongly, and evolution yields designs a human engineer would call stupid
(the backward retina) and cruelties that would cost nothing to remove, because there is no
one in the system to argue with. Evolution is closer to a god than to random noise, and
godlike in being bodiless, omnipresent and unmade, but it is mindless, divided and not
nice, Lovecraft's Azathoth; religionists who say they believe in a vague deity, or await
science's discovery of God, should have recognized it when Darwin found it.
