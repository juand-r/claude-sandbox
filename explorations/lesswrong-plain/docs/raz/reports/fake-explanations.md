# Report: fake-explanations

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A classroom joke: physics students, finding the side of a plate next to a radiator cool and
the far side warm, offer "heat conduction" and similar answers instead of saying "I don't
know"; the instructor had turned the plate around. An answer like "because of heat
conduction", used so that it could explain either outcome, controls no anticipation and is
no better than "because of magic"; the two differ only in literary genre. Real physics
would measure temperatures and use the diffusion equation, and the students' deeper error
was to think that saying science-sounding words is doing physics.
