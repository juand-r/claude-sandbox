# Report: schools-proliferating-without-evidence ("Schools Proliferating Without Evidence", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-15)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Drawing on Robyn Dawes's House of Cards, the post says psychotherapy is a field unbound from
evidence: the Rorschach survives despite failing hundreds of tests, therapy schools do not
differ in outcome, experience brings no gain, and talking to a college professor works as
well as seeing a therapist, yet therapists were licensed, paid and believed without
experimental support. Without evidence to decide between them, schools multiplied, because
prestige came from founding a school on a charismatic story rather than from a tested
technique. Hence any organized practice (the implied target is a rationality school) needs
measurement and controlled testing of its techniques.
