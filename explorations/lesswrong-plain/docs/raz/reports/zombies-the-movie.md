# Report: zombies-the-movie

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A mock film script: New York is overrun by philosophical zombies, who behave exactly as before but are not conscious, so the generals can see no difference, the "epiphenomenal virus" has no effects to study and no cure can work. David Chalmers, a victim, protests that this cannot happen, and Daniel Dennett, who admits to having no qualia, fights his way in to free him. The zombies answer that the humans are the ones with the virus, a colonel concludes he feels nothing, and the film goes black and silent and "continues exactly as before", which dramatizes the point of "Zombies! Zombies?" that removing epiphenomenal consciousness would change nothing that happens.
