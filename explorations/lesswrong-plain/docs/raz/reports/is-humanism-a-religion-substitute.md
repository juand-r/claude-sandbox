# Report: is-humanism-a-religion-substitute ("Is Humanism A Religion-Substitute?", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-03-26; book order 213)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The post argues that feeling transcendence at a rocket launch is not a substitute for
religion, even if it looked like religious feeling in a brain scan, because the desire behind
it is lawful and would exist in a world that never had religion. It grants that some atheists
do try to fill a religion-shaped hole, says their imitations ("hymns to the nonexistence of
God") are always bad, and proposes a test for post-theistic practice: would it make sense had
religion never existed? It ends that emotional investment in real things like space travel is
not vulnerable as belief in God is, because humanity actually exists.
