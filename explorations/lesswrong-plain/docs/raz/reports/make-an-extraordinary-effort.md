# Report: Make an Extraordinary Effort (order 314)

Agent: batch 6b. Slug: `make-an-extraordinary-effort`. Posted 2008-10-07.

## 1. The argument in three sentences

A "strong" effort gives mediocre results; a higher virtue is the Japanese isshokenmei, a
desperate all-out effort, which self-described rationalists need if they are to stop making
the mistakes that even Robert Aumann makes. But Japan, where that virtue is honoured, lags
the West, which values leaving the herd, so there is a higher and more dangerous virtue
still: the extraordinary effort, doing something out of the ordinary, which should be used
only for good reason but is too often ruled out by reflex. The author's own cases (Friendly
AI called futile, advice to earn degrees first) show people accepting the default outcome
without asking whether it could be changed, and the post asks for a sense of the cost of
ordinariness as well as of the risk of the extraordinary.
