# Report: replace-the-symbol-with-the-substance

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The rationalist version of Taboo (describing something without its usual word or any
synonym, and without coining a new handle) requires visualizing the details as if seeing
them for the first time, because a familiar word slides in and erases the details. This
matters because categories throw away information and purposes get lost when a symbol
(a degree, a test score) displaces the substance (learning): a mental category that
imperfectly matches a goal produces the same failure inside one head as a leaky proxy does
in an institution. The cure is to replace the symbol with the substance, as "The Simple
Truth" did for "truth" and Bayes's rule does for "evidence", and the most important word to
taboo is (unnamed) "rationality".
