# Report: go-forth-and-create-the-art ("Go Forth and Create the Art!", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-04-23)

Batch 6c, Book VI "Becoming Stronger", The Craft and the Community, book order 338 (the last
post of the book).

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The author has set down part of an art of rationality, developed in the course of personal
projects and weighted toward epistemic rationality, and says more of the art is absent than
present: akrasia, group coordination, training, teaching and verification among it. The
author guesses that there is a barrier to getting started, since people who set out to
develop an art of thinking go wrong by default (David Stove on Hegel, Freud, new religions),
and hopes that what has been written gives readers enough to avoid the worst errors and build
the rest. The task is left to readers, with two pieces of advice, hedged: draw on many
sources, and develop the art while trying to do something else that matters; and readers are
asked to go out into the world and report back to the community.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- The post is judged as an exhortation, per the brief. The notes are almost all descriptive,
  with credit where a fair reader needs it (the Stove attribution, the hedged second lesson).
  The Response is 331 words and mostly credit; the In short line has no charge. This is
  intended: I found no fault that survives the fair-defender test. Candidates I considered and
  dropped:
  - "That's what happens by default to anyone who sets out to develop the art of thinking" is
    unhedged, and its evidence is Stove's examples plus Freud. But the post labels the barrier
    a guess, attributes the view to Stove, and "by default" allows exceptions. Kept only as
    the neutral sentence in the Response ("not a survey, and the post does not claim more").
  - "Earlier I made efforts only in passing": the Twelve Virtues (2006) and other essays in
    this book were substantial efforts, but the post contrasts them with two years of daily
    posts ("This time I put some back into it"); memoir judged as memoir.
  - Asking readers not to get "all your rationality from one author" in a book by one author:
    the book is the editors' choice.
  - A note on the karma hope and LessWrong's later decline: no citable source found; also close
    to the "author's organizations" rule.
- CFAR is information only (the brief), kept out of the In short line, with founders from
  Wikipedia. The Response paragraph on it says "taken up"; check this is not read as a claim
  that CFAR succeeded.
- "The most-voted comments on that post were indeed critical of it." rests on current scores.
  If the editor thinks this is trivia, it can go without loss.
- Reserved words: "wrong" appears only in the post's own phrase "go wrong" (flagged five times
  by raz_check); it is not a verdict. "never" appears only inside the Stove quotation in this
  report.
- Pronouns: none for the author; Stove's "his" was restructured.
