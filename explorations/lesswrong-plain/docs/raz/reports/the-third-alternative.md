# Report: the-third-alternative ("The Third Alternative", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-05-06)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

A Noble Lie such as Santa Claus is usually defended by showing that it beats doing nothing,
which is a false dilemma: a policy better than the null action need not be the best, and a
Third Alternative (science fiction for wonder, praise instead of bribes) can usually supply
the benefit without the lie. Such false dilemmas persist because people who find a
convenient, defensible policy stop searching at once, while an inconvenient option makes
them keep looking. So beware of defending a policy as "defensible" rather than "optimal",
ask whether you would be glad to find a better option, and spend five minutes by the clock
looking for one.
