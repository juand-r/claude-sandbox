# Report: minds-an-introduction

## 1. The argument in three sentences

You are a mind running on a lawful brain that evolution built for reproduction in ancestral
environments, and you can study your own workings in order to improve them relative to your
own goals rather than evolution's. Our mental categories do not resemble physical ones, and
even after dualism is rejected our talk of "rationality" and "bias" can stay an unconstrained
"ghost"; so the book will use obviously inhuman purposeful-seeming processes, evolution and
artificial intelligence, as a mirror, and it previews Book III's sequences. The second half
profiles Yudkowsky's AI work (intelligence explosion, Friendly AI, existential risk, debated
and uncertain), and argues that AI belongs in a book on human rationality because it gives
simple illustrations of cognition and because long-term technology forecasting is an
important application of Bayesian rationality.
