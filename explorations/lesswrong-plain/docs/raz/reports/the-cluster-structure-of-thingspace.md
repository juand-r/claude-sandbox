# Report: the-cluster-structure-of-thingspace

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Any object can be pictured as a point in a "thingspace" with a dimension for every property,
and uncertainty about the object as a cloud of probability in that space. Categories such as
"mother" or "bird" are radial: the intension is a glow brightest at the typical centre, and the
extension is a cluster of points with dense central sub-clusters and dimmer outliers; the
author holds that the glow was learned from the clusters. Because real clusters need have no
set of necessary and sufficient properties, a definition only has to lead the listener to the
right cluster, and objecting to its exceptions is nitpicking.
