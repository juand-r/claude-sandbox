# Report: evaporative-cooling-of-group-beliefs ("Evaporative Cooling of Group Beliefs", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-12-07)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Groups often emerge from a crushing shock (a failed prophecy, a scandal) with stronger belief, and the usual explanation is individual cognitive dissonance. The post proposes a second, group-level mechanism modeled on evaporative cooling: the most skeptical members leave first, so the remaining group's consensus shifts toward its fanatics, and without moderates the remaining members may grow more fanatical still; When Prophecy Fails, the Objectivist split and a transhumanist mailing list are the illustrations. Hence groups should be slow to eject dissenters, though (Kuhn, and a footnote on Internet moderation) some exclusion of outsiders is needed to get work done.

## 3. Arithmetic

- Festinger timeline: failed midnight of 20/21 Dec 1954; Freund left after the 4:45 a.m. message, so about five hours later. Group gone by 9 January: 19 days ("within three weeks" in the Response).
- 2 of 11 Lake City members gave up the belief (book's count).
- Rand-Branden break 1968; Kelley split 1989-90: 21-22 years ("about twenty years").

## 5. Items not verified

- The Unarius landing date of 27 September 1975. Wikipedia and WRSP give a 1974 prediction, a 1976 Ladbrokes wager and 2001, not this date. A search result (a non-scholarly site) mentioned a September 1975 cancellation; I did not rely on it. The note says the date could not be confirmed.
- Melton (1985) and Dawson (1999) themselves; Kelly's earlier paper "Failed Prophecies Are Fatal" (2025). Quoted only as Kelly reports them.
- Kelly's archival claims are his; I have not seen the archives. The note attributes them to him ("argues").
- "The Ayn Rand Institute is (reportedly) more fanatical": hedged by the post; not checked.
- The mailing-list anecdote: unverifiable.
- Physics: "a potential energy barrier that's just a little higher than the average thermal energy". In practice evaporative cooling of atoms uses a cut several times kT, I believe, but I did not source it and the analogy does not depend on it. No note.

## 6. Judgment calls for the editor

- Reserved words: none used in notes, afterword or honest section.
- "surprised" note: limited to "the one study the post names"; a defender could say "early studiers" means others too.
- Kelly (2026) is recent and makes strong claims ("the book's central claims are false, and ... the authors knew"). I used only his factual findings, attributed ("argues"), and not the charge of bad faith. The editor may prefer to drop the archival sentence.
- The arithmetic note (paragraph 6) could look like a jab; its point is that the post's novel claim is the second, hedged one.

- Mailing-list cpara was rewritten after rereading: the post's own model includes ejection ("when a group ejects all its voices of moderation"), so I no longer say the anecdote shows a different mechanism, only that its departures were forced.
- Pronouns: he/him for Kurt Freund (the book's), Branden and Rand (historical), Hirschman.
