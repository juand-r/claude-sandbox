# Report: church-vs-taskforce ("Church vs. Taskforce", Yudkowsky, posted 2009-03-28)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

If religion disappeared, the author guesses, the largest gap it would leave is not in ideals or morals but in community, and many people who stay religious without quite believing may stay for their neighbours. Churches and friendly workplaces supply community only as a side effect, so a community built explicitly for community would be designed differently: time-shared buildings, welcome for newcomers, practices tested by experiment. The author proposes that rationalist communities form as local "taskforces" around shared work on the world's problems (or other shared goals such as learning), while granting that this is "probably" a dream.
