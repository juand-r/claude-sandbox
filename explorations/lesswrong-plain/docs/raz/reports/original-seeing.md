# Report: original-seeing

## 1. The argument in three sentences

The post is a passage from Robert Pirsig's Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance,
introduced by two sentences saying that the story is true whether or not it happened. In
the passage a student who cannot write about Bozeman, or about its main street, writes five
thousand words once told to start from one brick of one building. The teacher concludes she
was blocked because she was trying to repeat what she had heard, and narrowing the subject
forced her to see for herself.
