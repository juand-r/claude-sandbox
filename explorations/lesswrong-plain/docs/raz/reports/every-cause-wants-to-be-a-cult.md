# Report: every-cause-wants-to-be-a-cult ("Every Cause Wants To Be A Cult", Yudkowsky, posted 2007-12-12)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

The Register's allegations about Wikipedia's administrators show that a noble cause can turn cultish; the cause is ordinary human nature (in-group bias, happy death spirals), not a hidden flaw in the cause or its followers. Like heat flowing toward equilibrium, every group with an unusual goal drifts toward the "cult attractor" unless it spends constant effort resisting, and calling the cause "rationality" does not exempt it. Cultishness is a matter of degree, even in science (for example, whether journals favor famous authors), and a group that thinks only other groups' impurity made them cults will not make the effort.
