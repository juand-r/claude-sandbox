# Report: don-t-believe-you-ll-self-deceive

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Commenters such as Kurige say they practise "double-think" or chose to believe in God while
knowing the choice does not affect whether God exists, which puzzles the author, since
knowing one's map is not correlated with the territory should end the belief. The author
now grants that it may be quite easy not to make that jump, and that the earlier statement
"You cannot make yourself believe the sky is green by an act of will" was partly an attempt
to instill a self-fulfilling prophecy. So the author recommends repeating to oneself that
deliberate self-deception cannot work: believing in your own inability makes you less likely
to fool yourself.
