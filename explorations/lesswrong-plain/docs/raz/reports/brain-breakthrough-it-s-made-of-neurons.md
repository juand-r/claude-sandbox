# Report: brain-breakthrough-it-s-made-of-neurons ("Brain Breakthrough! It's Made of Neurons!", Yudkowsky, posted 2008-04-01; book order 221)

## 1. The argument in three sentences (written before annotating)

Posted on April 1st as an instance of the "Amazing Breakthrough Day" the author had proposed a
week earlier, the post is a mock press release reporting, as if new, the old discovery (Cajal,
Golgi's stain) that the brain is an enormously complicated network of cells. The release
gathers the history of neuroscience (Alcmaeon, Broca, Nemesius's ventricles, Galvani and
Helmholtz, Babbage's engine, Darwin) to argue that finding a complicated material system where
the mind should be is strong evidence that mind and body are one substance, that intelligence
is not ontologically fundamental, and that this weighs against every religion. Its joke is
that this is shocking news that has been available for a century, so that the reader should
face its implications as if it were announced today.
