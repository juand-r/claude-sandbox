# Report: mundane-magic

## 1. The argument in three sentences

Following "Bind Yourself to Reality," the author proposes a variant of the gratitude
list: list the abilities you have that would seem amazing if they were magic or if only a
few people had them. The post then describes binocular vision, speech, writing, hands and
tool-making, and sight in the grand language of fantasy powers, so that each ordinary
ability reads as a marvel. The last and longest item, the "Ultimate Power," is
intelligence: an inner model of the world used to steer toward chosen futures, the power
that dominates all others and that its bearers treat as the ground of moral worth, which
the post calls "a suspiciously self-serving argument."
