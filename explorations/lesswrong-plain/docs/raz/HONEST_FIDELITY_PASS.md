# Honest edition: fidelity and substance pass (user request, 30 September 2026)

The user: "Do one more pass, actually reading both the original post and the honest rewrite
to check that the honest rewrite captured the essence of the original (not necessarily all
details, but at least the important parts). In particular, a way to test this is: would
someone reading the rewrite learn the same as the original? I say this because I really
don't want to read the original. So the honest rewrite should not just be 'I talked about X'
but rather actually go into the detail of what X is (unless it's absolutely trivial and
irrelevant to the main point.) while you're at it please also check for correctness (your
rewrite should be faithful to the original)."

Method: for each post, in book order, read the original (data/originals/<slug>.md) in full,
then the honest section (honest/sections/<slug>.tex) in full. Then:

1. Substance. Would a reader of the rewrite learn what a reader of the post learns? Where
   the rewrite names an idea, example, argument or proposal without saying what it is
   ("I list five rules", "I describe my method", "I give three reasons"), state its content.
   Keep trivia out.
2. Fidelity. Every claim attributed to the author must match the post: no invented
   content, no wrong numbers, no claim stronger or weaker than the post's. Fix any error.
3. Keep our \nb notes as they are unless a change to the text makes one wrong or
   redundant.
4. Paraphrase in our words. Quotations stay short and verbatim (the repository is public;
   no long runs of post text). Check each new quotation against the original.
5. Style as before: plain, first person, no reporting tags, no metaphors of our own,
   no italics, no em dashes.

Edits are logged in docs/raz/changes/honest_fidelity_pass.md, with a one-line finding per
section. Sections that need nothing are crossed off with the finding "no change".
