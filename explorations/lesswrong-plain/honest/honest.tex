\section*{A note before starting}

Each section retells one post in its author's voice. It follows the post from start to finish and says plainly what each part does: what it claims, what it shows, and what it only appears to show. Where a paragraph contributes nothing to the argument, the retelling says so.

These are parodies written by the editor of \textsc{LessWrong, Annotated}, not the authors' words. Words inside quotation marks are the authors' own and have been checked against the originals. Every other claim about a post, including its errors, rests on the notes and Responses in the annotated edition, where the evidence is given.

Remarks in \textcolor{nbcolor}{dark red, marked n.b.,} are the editor's: corrections, facts from outside the post, and judgments. Everything else is in the author's voice, including the ironic lines.

The satire is meant to be fair. Where a post gets something right, the retelling reports it as faithfully as the rest.
