\honest{Mundane Magic}{Eliezer Yudkowsky}{2008}

As you may recall from some months earlier, I think a rationalist should bind their feelings to a lawful universe with no magic in it. \nb{The earlier post is ``Bind Yourself to Reality'' (March 2008), about seven months before this one. The book prints this post two places after it.}

There is an old trick against suffering: list the things you are grateful for. \nb{It has some support. In experiments by Emmons and McCullough (2003), people who kept such lists showed ``heightened well-being across several, though not all, of the outcome measures.''} So why not list the abilities you have that would be amazing if they were magic, or if only a few people had them? \nb{This variant has not been tested, and the post does not say it has.}

Suppose you had a magic second eye that let you see how far away things are, ``where an ordinary eye would see only a two-dimensional shadow of the true world.'' We will only appreciate binocular vision once it has a name like ``Mystic Eyes of Depth Perception.''

Then my list. Speech is Vibratory Telepathy. Writing is Psychometric Tracery, which lets the dead share their thoughts with us. The hands are Multidimensional Kinesis, which make tools that make finer tools. Sight is the Eye, which senses tiny vibrations in the Force that binds matter.

Last comes the Ultimate Power, which is intelligence: ``a smaller, imperfect echo of the entire universe,'' used to ``search out paths through probability to any desired future.'' \nb{This restates ``The Power of Intelligence'' (2007), which spoke of ``the power to search paths through the great web of causality.''} Nothing can oppose it except itself. It cannot be taken away ``without removing you.'' Those who lose it for good the Ultimates call ``schiavo,'' or ``dead while breathing.'' \nb{``Schiavo'' is Italian for ``slave.'' It appears to refer to Terri Schiavo, a woman in a permanent vegetative state whose care was fought over in American courts from 1998 to 2005.} The Ultimates say it is part of what makes a creature an end in itself, and that no one without it can understand why, which I call ``a suspiciously self-serving argument.''

``The users of this ability form an absolute aristocracy and treat all other life forms as their pawns.'' There the post ends. I do not come back to my list, or to the gratitude trick.
